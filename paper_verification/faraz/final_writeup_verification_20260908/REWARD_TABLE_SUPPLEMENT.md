## Recommended reporting format

Use **Raw configuration + Effective/logged behavior** as two distinct columns. Raw-only reporting hides the stage multipliers, frequency formulas, quality gates, aggregate-component stacking, total-reward caps, and TuneRLSft postprocessing. Effective-only reporting is also misleading because many values are conditional, frequency-dependent, or sums of several branches rather than fixed scalars. The paper should give the named raw constants (including thresholds, floors and caps) and then state the exact stage-specific formula or conditional logged behavior. Observed log values such as `0.28`, `0.09515141111111111`, `−0.055`, and `−0.11` should be labeled as validation examples, not configuration values.
